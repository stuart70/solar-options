"From paid power" is the share of the day's household use bought from the grid at full price. Free-window energy is left out, so on the free plan with a battery that carries the house, this can read 0%. "Solar used on site" is the share of solar output the home or battery uses rather than exporting. The representative day for each season is the one closest to that month's average solar output.
